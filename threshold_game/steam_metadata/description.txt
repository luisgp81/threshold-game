THRESHOLD: Global Crisis

The year is 2027. The world is fracturing.

You are the Director of a classified global intelligence agency. Six regions, five collapsing systems, and thirty weeks to prevent humanity's worst crisis since the Cold War.

THRESHOLD is a turn-based strategy game where every decision has consequences—some immediate, some delayed, all significant.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHAT YOU'RE MANAGING

Five critical agency resources—INTEL, STABILITY, FUNDS, INFLUENCE, and PUBLIC_TRUST—are all in constant decay. The world doesn't wait. Every week, every region pushes back against your control.

You'll manage six global regions:
• North Atlantic — NATO's fracturing alliances
• Eastern Bloc — Resurgent authoritarianism
• Middle Corridor — Humanitarian catastrophe
• Pacific Rim — Technological cold war
• Southern Front — Emerging power blocs
• Arctic Zone — [CLASSIFIED — Unlocks Week 10]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HOW YOU WIN (OR LOSE)

Three paths to victory:
→ DIPLOMATIC VICTORY: Stabilize global order through dialogue
→ INTELLIGENCE SUPREMACY: Build the world's most comprehensive intel network
→ PRAGMATIC CONTROL: Keep every system functional for 30 weeks

You lose if any single resource hits zero—or if three regions reach critical threat simultaneously.

FEATURES

• 80s military terminal aesthetic with authentic CRT scanline overlay
• 40+ unique geopolitical events across 5 categories
• Delayed consequence system — decisions echo 2-5 weeks later
• Director's Log — full record of every choice you make
• Declassified end-game report tailored to your playthrough
• Unlockable capabilities: BLACK OPS, AI ANALYSIS, ARCTIC ZONE
• Three difficulty levels: ANALYST / DIRECTOR / CRISIS MODE
• 90-120 minute playtime with high replayability
